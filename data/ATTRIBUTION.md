# Danuri learning-data attribution

Vocabulary levels: National Institute of Korean Language (NIKL), **2017 International Standard Korean Language Curriculum, vocabulary and grammar level lists**, revised 2020-11-17. [Original report and workbook](https://www.korean.go.kr/front/reportData/reportDataView.do?mn_id=45&pageIndex=5&report_seq=932&searchOrder=). KOGL Type 1 (attribution).

Dictionary glosses, English definitions, Korean sentence examples, grammar patterns, idioms, phrases and proverbs: **NIKL Korean Basic Dictionary**, full JSON download, October 2026. [Download source](https://krdict.korean.go.kr/download/downloadPopup), [copyright policy](https://krdict.korean.go.kr/kor/kboardPolicy/copyRightTermsInfo). Dictionary-derived text in `catalogue.js` and `nikl-catalogue.json` is offered under [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 2.0 Korea](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/kr/).

Changes by Danuri: normalized headwords; matched curriculum entries to dictionary headwords with part-of-speech and homonym checks; excluded unmatched vocabulary; grouped expression senses into one card per headword; selected the first sentence example; mapped dictionary-only beginner vocabulary to app Level 1; retained unmatched grammar and expressions as Ungraded. Examples without a paired translation have no example translation. Additional senses retain dictionary entry and sense identifiers in the downloadable catalogue.

These are learning materials for TOPIK preparation. Curriculum membership and dictionary inclusion do **not** prove appearance in a TOPIK exam. No verified past-exam occurrence is claimed in this release. Original project-authored seed cards and mini mock questions are separate supplementary practice, not reproduced past papers. No dictionary audio, pictures, or other media are redistributed.

When reusing or adapting dictionary-derived text, credit NIKL and Danuri, link the source and license, indicate changes, and share adaptations under the same license. The data license does not change the license of unrelated app code or original artwork.
